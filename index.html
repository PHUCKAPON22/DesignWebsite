The experience

Bangkok’s royal and spiritual heart, shared by someone who lives here.

This is more than a checklist of famous landmarks. Your local guide brings together the architecture, customs and everyday stories that help Bangkok’s sacred places make sense.

Begin inside the Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew, home to the highly revered Emerald Buddha. Continue to Wat Pho and its monumental Reclining Buddha before crossing the Chao Phraya River by local ferry to Wat Arun.

The pace is comfortable, the group is kept personal, and there is time to notice the details that are easy to miss when visiting alone.

“The temples are spectacular, but the small customs and stories are what help visitors understand why these places still matter today.”
Local guide note · WanderSiam Bangkok team

Your route

A thoughtful half-day through old Bangkok.

01

Grand Palace & Wat Phra Kaew

Meet near the river and begin inside Bangkok’s most important royal and sacred complex.

02

Wat Pho

Walk through one of the city’s oldest temple grounds and see the Reclining Buddha.

03

Local ferry & Wat Arun

Cross the Chao Phraya and finish below the porcelain-covered Temple of Dawn.

Practical details

Good to know.

What should I wear?

Shoulders and ankles must be covered when visiting the Grand Palace and temples. Comfortable walking shoes are recommended.

When will I receive confirmation?

You will receive confirmation by email with meeting instructions after your booking has been reviewed.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

You may reschedule or cancel for a full refund up to 24 hours before the tour start time.

What happens if it rains?

The tour operates rain or shine. Tropical showers are often brief, so a light raincoat or poncho is useful in the rainy season.
