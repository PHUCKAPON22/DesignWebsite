The experience

Bangkok’s landmarks, explained through local eyes.

This tour is more than a checklist of famous places. Your guide connects architecture, customs and everyday stories so Bangkok’s sacred sites feel meaningful rather than simply monumental.

Begin inside the Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew, continue to the Reclining Buddha at Wat Pho, then cross the Chao Phraya River by local ferry to Wat Arun. The pace leaves room for questions, photographs and the small details that are easy to miss when visiting alone.

“The small customs and stories are what help visitors understand why these places still matter today.”
Local guide note · WanderSiam Bangkok team

Your route

01

Grand Palace & Wat Phra Kaew

Wat Pho

03

Local ferry & Wat Arun

Cross the Chao Phraya and finish below the porcelain-covered Temple of Dawn.

Know before you book.

Dress code

Shoulders and ankles must be covered inside the Grand Palace and temples.

Entrance fees

Entrance fees are not included and must be paid in cash on the tour day.

When will I receive my confirmation?

You will receive confirmation and meeting instructions by email after the booking is reviewed.

Why are entrance fees not included in the tour price?

Entrance fees can change independently and are paid directly at each attraction.

What should I do if I might be late?

Contact WanderSiam as soon as possible using the details in your confirmation email.

Can I change my booking?

Contact the team before the cancellation deadline and we will check available alternatives.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel up to 24 hours before the tour for a full refund.

What if it rains on the day of the tour?

The tour normally runs rain or shine. A light raincoat is useful in the rainy season.

Under what circumstances might the itinerary change?

Opening hours, ceremonies, traffic or local conditions may require a responsible route adjustment.
